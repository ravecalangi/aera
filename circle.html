Reseller program

Pick your tier.

AERA Circle is our reseller program for individuals and businesses who want to share AERA with their own community. Applying and ordering are separate from regular retail shopping.

Starter

For first-time resellers and small side hustles.

  • Up to 15% off retail prices
  • Starter kit with product samples
  • Minimum order: ₱2,000

Grow

For active sellers building a steady customer base.

  • Up to 25% off retail prices
  • Priority restocks on new scents
  • Minimum order: ₱5,000

Partner

For stores, salons and bulk buyers.

  • Up to 35% off retail prices
  • Early access to new launches
  • Minimum order: ₱15,000

Ordering expectations

Apply through the form, and once approved you’ll receive the reseller price list. Orders are placed directly with our team, packed within 3–5 business days and paid in full before shipping via bank transfer or e-wallet.

Policy notes

Resellers agree to keep prices at or above AERA’s minimum advertised price, to use official product photos and descriptions, and not to make medical claims about any product.
